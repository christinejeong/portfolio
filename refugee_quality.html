Overview

Refugee Quality is a platform designed to help refugees adjust to new environments and feel a sense of belonging. It offers online courses, job opportunities, donation channels, and community spaces.

The website balances accessibility and visual clarity by combining colorful illustrations with clear typography, ensuring legibility for children and young users.

Background

In high school, I tutored three Syrian refugee children in English through the Refugee Administration Ministry in Korea. Their initial shyness inspired me to design a platform where refugees could feel cared for and supported while regaining access to their basic rights.

Research

My research identified two main focus areas:

1. Education

Refugees face systemic barriers to schooling. According to UNHCR, only 68% of refugee children attend primary school (vs. 91% globally), and just 6% have access to higher education. Girls face additional inequality due to cultural and social barriers.

2. Additional Needs

Interviews with Middle Eastern refugees in Korea highlighted three urgent challenges:

  • Employment opportunities
  • Access to basic supplies
  • Building a supportive community

The Solution

1. Education

Free online courses tailored to refugees' interests. The interface uses simple navigation and illustrations to make learning accessible for children.

2. Job Opportunities

Listings curated for refugees, connecting them with employers offering training and fair work.

3. Donations

A platform where users can donate essentials or funds directly to refugee families.

4. Community

A social space for refugees to share experiences and find peer support.

Final Design

The final website integrates courses, resources, donations, and community forums into a unified platform. Illustrations and a bright, child-friendly design enhance accessibility while ensuring usability.
