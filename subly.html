Overview
Subly is an intuitive subleasing platform tailored to the unique needs of college students, providing a safe, efficient, and streamlined process for subleasing and subletting apartments.

The platform allows users to post, find, and manage listings all in one place, making it simple to connect with trusted peers seeking or offering short-term housing. The platform also promotes trust through its user-friendly interface and clearly displayed recommended listings. Designed with student-centric features, Subly offers a smooth, reliable experience that transforms the traditionally complex process of subleasing into a quick, secure, and accessible solution for students across a wide range of universities.
Background
Finding housing as a student shouldn't feel like a gamble. Yet for many, it does.

During our early conversations with peers, one theme kept coming up: the subleasing process feels sketchy, unreliable, and unnecessarily stressful. From fake listings to endless Facebook group posts, students were piecing together housing options through word-of-mouth and scattered online platforms.

Subly was created to rethink that process, making subleasing safe, student-focused, and stress-free.
The Problem
How might we design a subleasing experience that feels trustworthy, straightforward, and tailored to student needs from start to finish?

Key challenges:
  • Verification gaps → Students struggled to know which listings (or subleasers) to trust.
  • Overwhelming searches → Hours wasted scrolling through cluttered, unfiltered housing boards.
  • Unclear process → Unorganized steps and unclear communication added to stress.
Research
To understand where the biggest frustrations occurred, I conducted user research through interviews and surveys.

Key insights:
  • Students preferred platforms with peer-to-peer credibility. Trust was higher when the source was another student.
  • Filtering tools were often inadequate, forcing students to spend unnecessary time browsing irrelevant listings.
  • The process felt mentally draining, leaving students anxious even after securing housing.
The Solution
We designed Subly as a student-only housing app that simplifies the subleasing journey through trust-building features and streamlined flows.

1. Verified student community
  • .edu email sign-up ensures a closed network of verified university students.
  • Builds credibility and lowers risk of scams.
2. Smart filtering system
  • Search by location, price, amenities, and availability.
  • Reduces information overload by surfacing the most relevant listings.
3. Guided onboarding
  • A quick, structured setup that reassures students while personalizing their housing search.
  • Sets the tone for a safer, more welcoming experience.

Takeaways
  • Trust is everything. Even small signals of credibility (like verification screens) made users feel more secure.
  • Less is more. A structured, low-pressure onboarding experience helped reduce initial overwhelm.
  • Personalization matters. Early setup made the platform feel more relevant and intuitive for different student needs.
